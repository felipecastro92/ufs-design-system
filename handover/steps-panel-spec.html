Steps Panel

The right column of a Recipe Detail page — locally called "Preparation": an intro paragraph, a title, and a numbered, ordered list of steps connected by a vertical line running from one index circle to the next.

Overview

Full Component Preview

The intro, title, and 3 connected steps — real copy reused from the recipe this panel documents (Meadowland Mash).

A rich, silky mashed potato built for high-volume service — MEADOWLAND Professional keeps it glossy and stable under a bain-marie, while Knorr Professional Chicken Stock Granules rounds out the seasoning without extra prep. Scales cleanly from a la carte to full banquet covers.

Preparation

  1. 1

    Cook the potatoes:

    • Peel and dice the potatoes into even 3cm chunks. Place in a large pot, cover with the water, add the salt and bring to the boil.
    • Simmer for 15–18 minutes until fork-tender, then drain well and return to the pot over low heat for 1 minute to steam off excess moisture.
  2. 2

    Make the mash:

    • Add the MEADOWLAND Professional and warmed milk, then mash until smooth, or pass through a ricer for a bulk-service finish.
    • Stir in the Knorr Professional Chicken Stock Granules, white pepper and nutmeg until fully combined.
  3. 3

    For serving:

    • Taste and adjust the seasoning, then finish with a generous scatter of chopped chives.
    • Hold in a bain-marie at 63°C or above until service, stirring occasionally to keep the texture smooth.

Anatomy & Rules

On the real Recipe Detail page this sits in the flexible column (grid-column:2) beside the Ingredients panel's fixed 406px column — shown here full-width and standalone, capped at 720px for a reading-friendly measure. The connecting line is drawn per-step (not once for the whole list), anchored to each .ufs-recipe-step's own content height so it always reaches exactly to the next circle regardless of how much text a step holds.

Responsive

Mobile Example

Below 768px the title steps down to --text-h4-mob-size, step subtitles to --text-h6-mob-size, and step list text to --text-body-sm-size. The intro paragraph additionally clamps to 3 lines with a working Read more/Read less toggle — on the real page this exact behavior lives on a sibling duplicate positioned above the Ingredients/Preparation tab switcher (so it stays visible regardless of which tab is active); documented here as this panel's own intro behavior.

Internationalisation

i18n & RTL

The intro and title mirror automatically (plain block text). The one thing that doesn't come for free is the connecting line — it's an absolutely positioned ::after pinned to left:16px, which stays on the physical left in RTL unless explicitly overridden, so an explicit [dir="rtl"] rule flips it to right:16px to stay under the (now-mirrored) index circles.

RTL

حساء بطاطس مهروسة ناعم مصمم للخدمة عالية الحجم — يحافظ ميدولاند بروفيشنال على لمعانه وثباته تحت البخار، بينما تضيف حبيبات مرق الدجاج من نور بروفيشنال نكهة متكاملة دون جهد إضافي. يتوسع بسهولة من الطلبات الفردية إلى تقديم المآدب الكاملة.

التحضير

  1. 1

    اطهِ البطاطس:

    • قشّر البطاطس وقطّعها إلى مكعبات متساوية 3 سم. ضعها في قدر كبير، غطّها بالماء، أضف الملح واتركها تغلي.
    • اترك البطاطس على نار هادئة 15-18 دقيقة حتى تنضج، ثم صفّها جيدًا وأعدها إلى القدر على نار خفيفة دقيقة واحدة للتخلص من الرطوبة الزائدة.
  2. 2

    حضّر الهريس:

    • أضف ميدولاند بروفيشنال والحليب الدافئ، ثم اهرس حتى يصبح ناعمًا، أو مرّره عبر مصفاة للحصول على قوام مناسب للخدمة بالجملة.
    • أضف حبيبات مرق الدجاج والفلفل الأبيض وجوزة الطيب واخلط جيدًا.
  3. 3

    للتقديم:

    • تذوّق واضبط التتبيل، ثم أنهِ الطبق برشّة سخية من الثوم المعمّر المفروم.
    • احتفظ بالطبق في حمام مائي بدرجة 63° مئوية أو أعلى حتى وقت التقديم، مع التحريك من حين لآخر للحفاظ على القوام الناعم.

Usage

Static markup for the desktop layout — no JS required. The mobile-only intro clamp needs one small toggle function, shown below.

// Mobile-only intro clamp — toggle-a-class-and-swap-the-label,
// same pattern as the FAQ accordion's own reveal toggle
function toggleIntroClamp(btn) {
  var intro = document.getElementById(btn.getAttribute('aria-controls'));
  var expanded = intro.classList.toggle('is-expanded');
  btn.textContent = expanded ? btn.dataset.lessLabel : btn.dataset.moreLabel;
  btn.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'true' : 'false');
}
💡
On the shipped Recipe Detail page, this toggle also scrolls the button back into view on collapse (btn.scrollIntoView()) so re-clamping a long intro doesn't leave the reader scrolled past it — omitted here since this doc's intro is short enough not to need it.
