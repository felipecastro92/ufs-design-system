Discover our full range of professional-grade products, built for foodservice kitchens. Our Knorr, Hellmann's and Carte d'Or ranges help you deliver consistent quality, cut prep time, and control costs across every dish on your menu.

If you would like nutrition information for any of our products, please contact us.

Plant-based Products – We support the plant-forward movement to include more plant-based foods and less animal products in the diet. Our guiding principle is that our plant-based products contain at least 80% plant-derived ingredients and up to 20% animal-derived ingredients.

Vegetarian Products – Our (ovo-lacto) vegetarian products may contain dairy products and eggs, but no other animal products.

Always check the label of all additional ingredients you use alongside these products to ensure they are free of all animal products.

Vegan Products – Our vegan products do not contain any animal products or animal by-products.

Always check the label of all additional ingredients you use alongside these products to ensure they are vegan.

Gluten-free Products – Our gluten-free products do not contain gluten, a protein found in many foods including wheat, rye, barley, etc.

Always check the label of all other ingredients you use alongside these products to ensure they are gluten-free.
